I am a philosophy PhD student at the University of Notre Dame in South Bend,
Indiana, where I work on medieval and Islamic philosophy.

I have been selected as a Gabriel Fellow through Notre Dame's Medieval
Institute, which supports independent pre-dissertation research.

## Research

My interests center on classical Islamic and Scholastic metaphysics and natural
philosophy. I plan to write on medieval theories of individuation and prime
matter, as well as on the reception of Averroist metaphysics in the later
medieval period.

Alongside my research I am working on Latin, paleography, classical Arabic, and
modern research languages.

## Background

Before returning to Notre Dame for my doctorate, I completed a BPhil at the
University of Oxford in 2021 and spent eighteen months working in higher
education in London. I received my BA in Philosophy, *summa cum laude*, from
Notre Dame in May 2019.

## Elsewhere

Outside of philosophy, I row on the St. Joseph River, practice yoga, and bake
for my colleagues.
